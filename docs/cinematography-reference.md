# Cinematography Reference

[Back to the README](../README.md)

This is a reference to cinematic choices used by Cinema. The app validates compatibility; available controls depend on current selections, medium, and era. A model cannot make physically faithful footage from a label alone.

For longer explanations, see [Cameras and Lenses](../CinemaPromptEngineering/Documentation/Cameras-Lense.md), [Camera Movement](../CinemaPromptEngineering/Documentation/Camera%20Movement.md), [Lights](../CinemaPromptEngineering/Documentation/Lights.md), and [Shot Compositions](../CinemaPromptEngineering/Documentation/Shot%20Compositions.md).

| Equipment | Notes |

Movement types are Static, Pan, Tilt, Pan & Tilt, Track In/Out, Push In, Pull Back, Truck Left/Right, Crab, Arc, Crane Up/Down, Boom Up/Down, Dolly Zoom, Push-Pull, Zoom In/Out, Crash Zoom, Roll, Whip Pan/Tilt, Follow, Lead, Orbit, Reveal, and Fly Through. Timing choices are Static, Very Slow, Slow, Moderate, Fast, and Whip Fast.

## Shot sizes and composition

Shot sizes are Extreme Wide Shot (EWS), Wide Shot (WS), Medium Wide Shot (MWS), Medium Shot (MS), Medium Close-Up (MCU), Close-Up (CU), Big Close-Up (BCU), Extreme Close-Up (ECU), Over the Shoulder (OTS), POV, American Shot, and Italian Shot.

Composition choices include Rule of Thirds, Centered, Symmetrical, Golden Ratio, Golden Spiral, Dynamic Symmetry, Leading Lines, Frame Within Frame, Negative Space, and Depth Layering, among others. Compatibility rules can restrict composition choices for a selected shot size or medium; consult the [Shot Compositions](../CinemaPromptEngineering/Documentation/Shot%20Compositions.md) reference for the full explanation.

## Related controls

Color-tone options include Warm Saturated/Desaturated, Cool Saturated/Desaturated, Neutral Saturated/Desaturated, Monochrome, Sepia, Teal & Orange, Cross Processed, Bleach Bypass, and High/Low Contrast B&W. Mood choices are grouped into positive, neutral, tension, dark, and intense categories.
